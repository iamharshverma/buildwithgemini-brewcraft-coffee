# My agent: AI-Powered Personal Portfolio Concierge
One-liner: A conversational agent that helps visitors explore Harsh Verma’s portfolio—blogs, talks, awards, and services—while enabling bookings, purchases, and personalized discovery of content and expertise.

Tool coverage:

Memory: User interests (e.g., AI, cybersecurity, startups), previously viewed content (blogs, talks, awards), booking history (1:1 sessions), and purchase history (books, services).
Tools: Content retrieval (blogs, articles, talks, awards, research), booking & scheduling (calendar integration for 1:1 sessions and speaking requests), payment processing (books, services), and contact/lead submission.
Catalog/UI: Portfolio rendered as card-based UI including blog cards (title, summary, tags), talk cards (event, topic, links), award cards (title, year), book cards (cover, buy option), and service cards (offerings, pricing).
Image gen: Custom visuals for blog covers, award highlights, book promotions, and journey storytelling assets.
Sandbox: Booking availability logic, pricing calculations (bundles/discounts), personalized recommendations, and content ranking (latest, trending, relevant).

Core rails (everyone): memory, tools, eval, deploy, frontend
My stretch menu (pick later): A2UI portfolio cards, image generation, multi-agent workflows (content + booking + commerce), voice assistant
First eval question: "Show me Harsh Verma’s latest AI security blogs and help me book a 30-minute consultation."
